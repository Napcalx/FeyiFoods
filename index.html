Why Jaggery?

Sweetness, Naturally Different

Jaggery is a traditional sweetener made by slowly boiling down the juice of sugarcane (or palm sap) until it sets into a golden-brown block. People across Africa, Asia and beyond have sweetened tea, porridge and sweets with it for centuries.

Unlike refined sugar, which is stripped down to plain white crystals, jaggery keeps its natural molasses, so it is minimally processed, with a deep caramel flavour. Our cube format makes it easy: drop one into a hot drink, grate it into a bake, or snack on it as it is. No chipping, no mess.

Refined Sugar

Jaggery Cubes

Jaggery is still a sweetener, so enjoy it mindfully as part of a balanced diet.
